\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

\sumpara Three things are now known about sequencing this line. \sumparaend
\fin On exact data, iterated greedy lowered total weighted tardiness relative to the earliest due date rule by 81.0 to 98.5~percent on the development instances and reached the proven optimum on both instances for which we certified one. \fine
\fin Held fixed while processing times varied at a coefficient of variation of 0.2, its plan had the lower weighted tardiness in all 30~scenarios and an 88.1~percent lower mean. \fine
\fin As variability rose to a coefficient of 0.8, the relative reduction fell to 27.2~percent and the 10th percentile of the per-period reduction to 851.5 weighted minutes, while the mean absolute reduction stayed between 2,572.0 and 3,683.7 weighted minutes. \fine

\sumpara The contribution is evidence on how variability changes the value of a searched plan. \sumparaend
\fin For a planner who must fix one order before the work starts, we found a searched sequence whose mean gain over the due-date rule held at every level of processing-time variability we tested, and variability, the one factor we varied, decided how dependably that gain arrived in a single period. \fine
