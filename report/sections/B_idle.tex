\section{Idle time by stage}\label{sec:idle}

\sumpara Idle time differs across sequences only through their makespans. \sumparaend
\fin \Cref{tab:idle} reports the idle time of every stage for each schedule of \cref{tab:comparison}. \fine
\fin A stage's idle time is the makespan minus the stage's total processing time, so it includes any time before the stage starts its first job. \fine
\fin Because a stage's total processing time is the same for every sequence, two schedules of an instance differ in idle time at every stage by the difference in their makespans. \fine
